# Professor Guidance and Team Interpretation

## Guidance Received

The professor advised the team to use the model provider and orchestration framework that are most feasible and to do the best implementation possible.

The professor also suggested proving integration between two Subject Matter Expert agents first and then expanding the pattern to all four domains.

## Team Interpretation

- Local Ollama inference is acceptable for the prototype.
- Azure credits are not required for the selected implementation.
- Microsoft Agent Framework is not mandatory.
- LangGraph is selected because it is feasible for the team and the 24-hour schedule.
- Technology and SEC will be integrated first.
- Clinical and Corporate Responsibility will reuse the validated specialist pattern.
- All four domains remain part of the target architecture.

## Assumptions Still in Effect

- The application may be demonstrated locally unless the professor later requires deployment.
- The course data will not be committed to GitHub unless permission is provided.
- The project will deliver a working demonstration, source repository, architecture explanation, and evaluation evidence.
- Recommendations remain advisory and require human review for clinical, financial, or procurement decisions.
